About Blunova Properties

Your trusted partner in finding quality rental homes in Knoxville, Tennessee. Since 2010, we've been dedicated to providing exceptional living experiences for our tenants.

Our Story

Blunova Properties was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to provide quality rental homes in Knoxville that people would be proud to call home. What started as a small family-owned business has grown into one of Knoxville's most trusted property management companies.

Our founders, long-time Knoxville residents, saw an opportunity to raise the standards in the rental market. They believed that tenants deserved better—better properties, better service, and better overall rental experiences. This belief continues to drive everything we do today.

Over the years, we've expanded our portfolio to include some of the most desirable rental properties in Knoxville's sought-after neighborhoods. Each property in our portfolio is carefully selected and meticulously maintained to ensure our tenants enjoy the highest quality of life.

Our Mission

At Blunova Properties, our mission is to provide exceptional rental experiences through quality homes, transparent processes, and responsive service. We strive to create communities where our tenants feel valued, respected, and proud to call their rental property home.
