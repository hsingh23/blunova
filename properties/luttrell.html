Property Overview

Welcome to Luttrell—a charming single-family home that embodies the warmth and character of traditional Knoxville living. This thoughtfully maintained property features 3 cozy bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, offering comfortable living spaces perfect for families or individuals seeking an affordable housing option without compromising on quality.

The home's traditional design elements create a welcoming atmosphere from the moment you step inside. The classic layout provides distinct living areas that allow for both family gatherings and private relaxation. The kitchen, while maintaining its traditional charm, has been updated with modern appliances to ensure convenience for today's lifestyle.

Character & Comfort

Luttrell combines timeless architectural features with practical modern amenities. Hardwood floors, custom moldings, and traditional fixtures throughout the home create a sense of permanence and quality. The spacious rooms are filled with natural light, creating bright, inviting spaces that feel both expansive and cozy at the same time.

Community & Location

Located in one of Knoxville's established neighborhoods, Luttrell offers the benefits of a tight-knit community with easy access to urban conveniences. The neighborhood's tree-lined streets and well-maintained properties reflect the pride residents take in their community.
